HOW TO PLAY

Setup

One player creates a game and shares the 4-letter room code. The other player joins using the code.

Place Ships

Tap a ship from the fleet list, then tap the grid to place it. Tap the rotate button to change orientation. Place all 5 ships and hit READY.

Battle

Take turns firing at the enemy grid. Tap a cell to target, then tap FIRE. Hits glow red, misses show blue splashes. Sink all 5 enemy ships to win!
